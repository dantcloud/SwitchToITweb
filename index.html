Whether you’re stuck in a job with limited progression, returning to work, or simply looking for a new challenge, a career in IT could be closer than you think. At Switch to IT, we help people from all backgrounds gain the knowledge, confidence, and strategy needed to secure their first IT role. No technical background? No problem. Many successful IT professionals started with no experience. The key is having the right guidance and a clear plan.

IT Career Coaching

Breaking into IT can feel overwhelming when you don’t know where to start.

Our coaching programme provides personalised guidance to help you:

  • Present your transferable skills effectively
  • Build the technical knowledge, practical skills and confidence employers look for in entry-level IT candidates
  • Understand the IT industry and available career paths
  • Build a practical learning roadmap
  • Create a professional CV and LinkedIn profile
  • Build interview confidence through mock interviews and personalised feedback
  • Gain hands-on experience by working on real-life scenarios
  • Learn how to apply for entry-level IT roles effectively
  • Avoid common mistakes that slow down career changers
  • Job Search & Interview Support

You’ll have a clear action plan and ongoing support throughout your journey.

Why Choose Switch to IT?

Many people believe they are too late, lack experience, or do not have the right qualifications to start a career in IT.

The reality is that employers hire people from a wide range of backgrounds every day. With the right guidance, preparation, and mindset, breaking into the IT industry is more achievable than many people think.

At Switch to IT, we focus on practical advice, honest guidance, and proven strategies to help you take your first steps into a career in technology.

From learning the right skills and building confidence to preparing for interviews and understanding the job market, we support you throughout the entire journey - right up until you secure your first IT role.

About Us

At Switch to IT, our team consists of experienced IT professionals who actively work in the industry every day. Our coaching sessions are delivered on weekday evenings and weekends, making it easier to learn around your current commitments.

This means you will receive guidance, coaching, and practical advice from IT engineers who understand the real challenges of the industry because they are living it day to day. Every coach started exactly where you are now - taking the first step into an IT career.

We focus on giving you up-to-date, real-world knowledge that reflects the current IT job market. From technical skills and career guidance to interview preparation, we help you build the confidence and experience needed to succeed in real entry-level IT interviews.

Our goal is simple: to help you land your first IT role and open the door to long-term career progression in the area of technology that interests you most.
